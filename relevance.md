# Relevance criteria

A concrete government, parliamentary or EU step on something my proposals track:
- The KRITIS identification ordinance (KritisV): a draft, issuance, a timeline or a delay.
- Draft bills or cabinet decisions modernising the civil protection act (ZSKG), the supply-assurance laws, or creating the health supply-assurance act.
- Federal budget decisions on civil defence, including the scope of the debt-brake exemption and funding under the Pact for Civil Protection.
