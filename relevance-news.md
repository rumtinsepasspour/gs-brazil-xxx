# News alert relevance criteria

Items are scored High, Medium, or Low. High items appear in Highlights.

All High and Medium items must have a clear link to Germany (German federal or state government, German institutions, Germany's role in EU policy, or Germany as the primary subject of analysis). Items about other countries with no German angle are Low.

## High

Policy or government relevance required. Any one of the following qualifies:
- Concrete steps by the German government, parliament, or EU on civil defence or critical infrastructure: legislation, cabinet decisions, parliamentary questions, budget decisions, ordinances.
- Statements or positions from federal or state ministries, the interior ministers' conference (IMK), the state premiers' conference (MPK), or EU institutions specifically addressing Germany.
- Publication of official German documents: national risk analyses, the shelter concept, the KRITIS resilience strategy, audit reports, official evaluations.
- Sabotage, hybrid attacks, or major incidents affecting German critical infrastructure.
- Substantive expert or think-tank analysis of German civil defence or KRITIS policy.
- Positions from German industry bodies, aid organisations, or municipal associations on civil defence law or funding.

## Medium

The search term must be the main subject of the item, and the item must have a clear German connection. Policy relevance is not required. Any one of the following qualifies:
- Reporting on German civil defence exercises, emergency drills, or infrastructure incidents where the event itself is the story.
- Local or regional German news where the search term is the clear focus.
- Analysis or reporting on how Germany is affected by or responding to the alert topic (e.g. a risk analysis of Germany's exposure to climate change, a study of Germany's pandemic preparedness, a report on biosecurity risks in Germany).
- General explainers or background features about German civil defence or critical infrastructure where the search term is the central topic.

## Low

- Items where the search term appears in passing or as context for a different story.
- Items about Switzerland, Austria, or other countries with no German angle.
- Law-firm and consultancy compliance briefings.
- Job adverts, product marketing, event listings, conference announcements.
- Items where the search term is used in an unrelated sense.
